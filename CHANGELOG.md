# Changelog

## 1.5.1

Initial public release.

- Record the microphone, Windows system audio, or both.
- Transcribe locally with SenseVoice in Chinese or automatic mode.
- Group turns by voice with bundled CAM++ embeddings; unconfirmed turns stay editable.
- Rich notebook with bold, color, headings, bulleted/numbered lists, and timestamps.
- Meeting history, local archives, and generated minutes.
- Bundled speaker models retain their Apache-2.0 and MIT notices.
